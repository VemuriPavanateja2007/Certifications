A curated collection of certificates and course completion records earned through **Infosys Springboard** — Infosys' digital learning platform for building skills across AI/ML, programming, data science, and emerging technologies.

This repository serves as a personal portfolio and proof-of-learning archive, documenting continuous upskilling in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AIML) and related domains.

---

## 📌 About

Infosys Springboard offers free, industry-relevant courses across technology and life skills, curated in partnership with global educational institutions. This repository consolidates **23 certificates** earned throughout the learning journey, making them easy to reference, share, and verify.
